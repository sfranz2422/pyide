A+ labs

A lab is a computer with something wrong with it, from a support ticket. Students fix it on a bench of real parts and on a Windows-style screen with its tools and Command Prompt. Each task is checked on the server as they work, their work is turned in for them, and the points go into the grade.

Setting one

  • On a class page, Add to class → Lab (A+), then Assign beside the ticket. It goes on top of the class's page, named with its number: Lab 3 · Make a new drive usable.
  • Try it first (on the tickets page, or Try it on the lab's own page): what you do there is kept in your browser and turned in nowhere.
  • Labs are never taught live.
  • The sandbox is open to anyone, with every part and nothing checked. Start in Windows skips the building, for practising the tools.

Its notes

Every lab has notes beside it: a tutorial in your library, started from the ticket. Assign the same ticket to another class and it shares the same tutorial, so you write it once. Questions in it count toward the lab's grade, the same as any notes.

A lab block names some of the ticket's tasks, one a line, and shows as a card the student ticks by fixing the computer:

```lab
power
monitor
```

With locked: yes as a line at the top of the notes, a slide with a card holds the next slide until everything on its card is done: the way to walk a class through a ticket a step at a time.
