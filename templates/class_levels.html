Suggested levels

Each student sees your suggestion with a ⭐ on the level slide before a coding exercise and a challenge. They earn the exercise's points (and the challenge's bonus point) only at your suggested level, and not at all if they change level. They can still pick another level unless you lock it 🔒. With no suggestion, the level they choose counts if they keep it. Easy counts the exercise's points 80% (B-), Medium 88% (B+), Master 100%.

Students see: “⭐ {{ teacher_name or "Your teacher" }} suggests Medium for you.”
