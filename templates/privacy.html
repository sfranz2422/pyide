Privacy

Privacy policy

For PyIDE (pyide-mdfd.onrender.com), WebIDE (webide-4kiy.onrender.com) and FlaskIDE (flaskide.onrender.com). Last updated October 2, 2026.

These are browser-based coding editors that Steve Franz, a teacher, runs for programming classes. Your code runs in your own browser; the server stores and serves it and never runs it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Without signing in

You can use the editors without an account. Nothing about you is stored on the server unless you press Share. A share saves the code, the project name and whatever name you typed in the name box, so that anyone with the link can open it. Work you haven't shared is kept only in your own browser's storage.

Signing in with Google

Signing in asks Google for your name, email address and Google account ID. Nothing else from your Google account is requested. With them the editors store:

  • your name and email, so your work is saved to you and your teacher can see who turned it in;
  • the projects you save, including assignment work, which saves itself as you type;
  • work you turn in, with the time, and any feedback your teacher writes on it;
  • during a live lesson, the code your teacher is showing the class.

Your teacher can see work turned in to their assignments, and the names of students who opened them. Nobody else can see your saved work unless you share a link to it.

Google Classroom (teachers only)

A teacher can choose to connect their Google Classroom so the editors can list their classes, post assignments to them, look up the students on their class rosters (names and email addresses, to match students to their work), and send grades back. Students are never asked for Classroom access.

To do this the editors keep a Google access token for that teacher, encrypted. It is used only for those purposes, only when the teacher acts, and is deleted when they press Disconnect; they can also remove access at any time from their Google account's security settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where it is kept, and who else is involved

  • The editors and their database are hosted by Render, which keeps ordinary request logs (including IP addresses) for a short time.
  • Pages load code and fonts from public content networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts), which see your IP address as any website would.
  • The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Deleting your information

You can delete your own saved projects from My work. Work you turned in stays with your teacher. To have your account and everything attached to it deleted, email the address below.
